Introduction

About me

Research at the intersection of theory and implementation

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust (SnT), University of Luxembourg. My research focuses on signal processing and physical-layer wireless communications, particularly satellite and non-terrestrial networks.

My work spans multi-satellite diversity, space–time block coding, receiver synchronization and equalization, digital and distributed beamforming, and multi-antenna signal processing. In parallel, I study link reliability through realistic propagation and interference modelling, stochastic geometry, and finite-blocklength analysis.

I earned my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from IIT Indore in 2021 and my M.Tech. in Telecommunication Systems Engineering from IIT Kharagpur in 2015.

Research interests

Research directions

A focus on reliable, practical wireless and satellite systems, from algorithm design to analytical performance evaluation.

01 / DIVERSITY

Multi-satellite reception

Cooperative and diversity-enabled links, space–time block coding, combining under timing mismatch, and robust user-terminal receiver design.

02 / SIGNAL PROCESSING

Digital & distributed beamforming

Array processing, non-uniform beam and array lattices, low-complexity architecture choices, and implementation constraints.

03 / COMMUNICATION THEORY

Short-packet reliability

Finite-blocklength performance, realistic satellite fading, interference-limited systems, and multiuser resource sharing.

04 / RECEIVERS

Synchronization & estimation

Time alignment, channel estimation, equalization, linear combining, and robust physical-layer processing.

05 / NTN

Satellite–terrestrial integration

Satellite–terrestrial network models, cognitive spectrum sharing, cooperative relaying, and practical propagation impairments.

06 / ADVANCED LINKS

NOMA & RIS-assisted systems

Multiuser communication, interference cancellation, finite-resolution phase control, and realistic reliability analysis.
